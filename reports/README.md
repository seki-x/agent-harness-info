# Weekly Reports

This directory contains human-facing research reports. Reports are not
canonical knowledge and must not be used as evidence for canonical claims.

Weekly reports live under `reports/weekly/` and answer:

    What happened this week that is worth knowing?

This differs from canonical knowledge, which explains what should currently be
understood, and from the changelog, which explains how and why the canonical
knowledge state changed.

Reports may discuss WATCH and important NO_OP findings. They must distinguish
established changes from emerging signals and uncertainty, link directly to
important primary sources, and avoid manufacturing novelty during quiet weeks.

Published reports are historical records. Correct clear factual errors when
necessary, but do not rewrite old reports merely to match later understanding.
They are rendered by the Docusaurus blog plugin and are not part of monthly
knowledge versioning.
