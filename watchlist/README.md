# Watchlist

The watchlist stores ideas that may become important but do not yet have
sufficient evidence to enter canonical knowledge. It is intentionally outside
knowledge/.

## Use the watchlist when

- a new pattern appears potentially important;
- adoption is narrow;
- terminology is unstable;
- evidence comes primarily from one ecosystem;
- the long-term conceptual boundary is unclear.

## Suggested entry format

    # Candidate name

    First seen: YYYY-MM-DD
    Last reviewed: YYYY-MM-DD
    Status: watching

    ## Why it may matter

    ...

    ## Evidence

    ...

    ## What would justify promotion

    ...

    ## What would justify removal

    ...

An item may become canonical emerging or core knowledge, merge into an
existing concept, or be removed when the signal fails to develop. Watchlist
presence is not endorsement.
